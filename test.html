The assay

Enavima CMV point-of-care test

A rapid nucleic acid test for cytomegalovirus, designed for the newborn nursery rather than the reference laboratory, because the window that matters closes before a send-out comes back.

How it works

Three steps, one temperature

1. Collect

A dried blood spot, saliva swab, urine swab or lesion swab, whichever specimen your protocol already uses. No dedicated collection device to introduce.

2. Amplify

Isothermal amplification of CMV nucleic acid at a single constant temperature. No thermocycler and no temperature ramping, which is what removes the need for a molecular laboratory.

3. Read

Fluorescent lamp observation, or lateral flow where no instrument is available. Result in approximately 30 minutes from sample to answer.
